## 2024-05-24 - Actionable Emergency Contacts
**Learning:** In markdown-only repositories focused on crisis resources, standard text for emergency numbers introduces a high-friction barrier. Users under cognitive load or distress, particularly on mobile devices, benefit significantly from one-tap interactions.
**Action:** Always format phone numbers and SMS shortcodes as actionable `tel:` and `sms:` URIs in crisis or emergency documentation to reduce cognitive load and save time.
