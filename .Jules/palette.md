## 2026-08-10 - Markdown Emergency Resources Needs Clickable Links
**Learning:** In markdown-based guides (especially those dealing with mental health or crises), providing plain text phone numbers is not sufficient. Users on mobile devices need to be able to immediately call or text emergency services. The standard `[Text](tel:number)` and `[Text](sms:number)` URL schemes work in markdown.
**Action:** When adding or reviewing emergency contacts in markdown, always ensure they are wrapped in actionable `tel:` or `sms:` links to reduce friction during high-stress moments.
