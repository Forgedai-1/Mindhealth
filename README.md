# Mindhealth: A Guide for Those Living With Someone Experiencing Psychosis

## About This Resource

**Mindhealth** is a comprehensive guide designed specifically for people living with someone experiencing psychosis. If your spouse, family member, friend, or roommate is going through psychosis, this resource helps YOU understand what's happening, how to react, what to do, and how to cope with the stress and emotional toll.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

**I am NOT a medical doctor and cannot prescribe treatment or medications.** This resource provides educational information and practical support strategies based on caregiver experiences and widely-accepted best practices. All information is offered for your discretion. Please discuss concerns with qualified healthcare professionals.

## Key Points to Remember

✅ **This is NOT your fault** - You didn't cause it, you can't fix it alone, but you CAN help

✅ **Your loved one is not "crazy"** - They're experiencing a treatable medical condition

✅ **You matter too** - Taking care of yourself is essential, not selfish

✅ **Help is available** - Professional support works when people seek it early

✅ **Recovery is possible** - Many people get significantly better with treatment

## Emergency Resources

If you or your loved one is in immediate danger:

- **🚑 Call 911** (US) or your local emergency number
- **☎️ National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988**
- **💬 Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741**
- **🆘 NAMI Helpline: 1-800-950-NAMI (6264)**
